Trip 01

Azure Bay

From 65 BGN · May–Oct · 10–12 hours

A postcard-perfect bay on the coast

Hidden between rocky hills and Mediterranean greenery, Azure Bay is the kind of place you dream about long before you see it. The water shifts from turquoise to deep blue, and the white seabed gives the beach a tropical feel.

The trip includes a quiet harbor town and a local market. Enjoy fresh seafood with a view, then take a relaxed walk along the coast.

Bring comfortable shoes and sun protection. Swimming and snorkeling are best from late May to September.

Forest Haven

From 55 BGN · Mar–Nov · 8–9 hours

Ancient woods and rushing waterfalls

Forest Haven is a nature reserve with ancient beech and oak forests, mountain streams and a chain of waterfalls reaching 18 meters.

The route is designed for strong views with moderate effort. Ideal for nature lovers and photographers.

Wear waterproof shoes and a light rain jacket. Recommended for children 6+.
