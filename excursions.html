Trip 06

Day Tour to Nis, Serbia

From 120 EUR · Year-round · 10 hours

Rome, empire and resistance, across the border

Nis gave the world Constantine the Great, and its layered history runs from the Roman era through Ottoman rule to the 20th century. We start at Medijana, a Roman archaeological park, then visit the Skull Tower — a haunting monument built by the Ottomans from the skulls of Serbian and Bulgarian rebels.

The Nazi Concentration Camp, remarkably preserved and the site of a successful 1942 escape, brings the 20th century into focus, before we end at Nis Fortress and a relaxed lunch of traditional Serbian food in the old town.

Trip 07

Skopje & Matka Canyon

From 190 EUR · Apr–Oct · 12+ hours

Two capitals' worth of history, one canyon of calm

North Macedonia's capital is a study in contrasts — the authentic Ottoman-era Old Bazaar sits across the river from the neo-classical new center built under the "Skopje 2014" project. A local Macedonian guide joins us for two hours to share a perspective our own guide can't.

We walk the Old Fortress, the 15th-century Sultan Murat Mosque, and cross the centuries-old Stone Bridge before reaching Macedonia Square and the Gate Macedonia triumphal arch. In the afternoon we trade history for nature at Matka Canyon, with time for a boat ride past one of the deepest underwater caves in the world.

Trip 08

Skopje — Small Group

From 100 EUR · Year-round · 12 hours

Skopje, unhurried and in small company

Tick off another Balkan capital in a day, with two professional guides — one Bulgarian, one local Macedonian — sharing both sides of the story. Skopje's mix of Ottoman-era monuments and the ornate neo-classical buildings of the "Skopje 2014" rebuild makes it one of the more surprising cities in the region.

Highlights include the Old Fortress, the Sultan Murat Mosque, the Old Bazaar's historic caravanserais, and the new Macedonia Square with its monument to Alexander the Great. We also stop at the Mother Teresa Memorial House, dedicated to Skopje's most famous native, before finishing at the Gate Macedonia arch.

Trip 09

Lovech, Devetashka Cave & Krushuna Waterfalls

From 120 EUR · Year-round · 8+ hours

A historic town, a cave, and turquoise cascades

One of Bulgaria's continuously inhabited towns, Lovech's old quarter rewards a slow walk through cobbled streets past 19th-century houses, the Vassil Levski Museum, the Hisarya Fortress, and the Covered Bridge — a 19th-century landmark built by master craftsman Kolyo Ficheto.

From there it's a short drive to Devetashka Cave, a vast karst chamber once used as a military base, and on to the turquoise, terraced pools of the Krushuna waterfalls — a walk through forest and cascading water that closes out the day.
